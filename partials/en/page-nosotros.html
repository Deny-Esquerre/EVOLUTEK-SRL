A Peruvian industrial technology company

EVOLUTEK SRL is a Peruvian company focused on industrial technology: automation, IoT, Industry 4.0 and hydrocarbon fiscal metering.

We connect the world of industrial operations (OT) with information technology (IT) to capture, integrate, contextualize, historize, visualize and analyze data from industrial processes.

We offer a combination of industrial automation, IoT, SCADA, data historians, analytics, maintenance and industrial digital transformation, with solutions tailored to each client’s needs.

Our team of professionals has more than 25 years of experience in automation, industry and digital transformation, and we support our clients throughout the entire project lifecycle: assessment, architecture design, implementation, commissioning, training and ongoing support.

We work with leading technologies and a network of strategic partners that enable us to deliver robust, scalable solutions adapted to the reality of each operation, generating measurable results that last over time.
