Data Integration

Flow Software is an industrial data integration and movement platform that simplifies information management in manufacturing. It connects sources such as OPC servers, IoT gateways, historians, databases and LIMS, QMS or ERP systems, and processes that information through modeling and execution engines to turn raw data into actionable information.

Its approach rests on four pillars: Data Management, Information Modeling, Intelligent Execution Engines and Universal Information Access. With reusable templates and metrics, Flow aggregates and contextualizes data from multiple plants, calculates KPIs and makes them available to users, dashboards and machine learning models, while ensuring security, data governance and a continuous, reliable flow between OT and IT.

  • Connectivity to OT sources, databases and IT systems.
  • Inventory of the sources, metrics and KPIs to integrate.
