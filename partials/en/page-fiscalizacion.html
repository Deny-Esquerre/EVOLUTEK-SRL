Hydrocarbon Fiscal Metering

Preventive and corrective maintenance of gas fiscal metering points and LACT units. Specialists in flow computers, Coriolis and ultrasonic meters, and gas chromatographs.

We design, install and commission gas and liquid metering points to international standards, and support them throughout their service life with calibrations, verifications and local technical support from Talara.

We also integrate fiscal metering into SCADA systems to monitor every point in real time, enable remote auditing and anticipate failures with artificial intelligence.

  2. Stage 02

    Maintenance and asset management

  3. Stage 03

    Digitalization and SCADA

    Integration of fiscal metering systems for real-time monitoring, remote auditing and AI-powered monitoring.
