What we offer

Evolutek SRL is a Peruvian technology company specializing in Industrial Automation, IIoT (Industrial Internet of Things), Industry 4.0 and Hydrocarbon Fiscal Metering Points. What is IIoT?

IIoT, or the Industrial Internet of Things, connects machines, sensors and devices across industries to collect and analyze data in real time. With IIoT, companies can optimize their processes, solve problems before they escalate and improve operational efficiency. This technology turns data into valuable information, helping you make smart decisions and stay competitive in today’s market.
